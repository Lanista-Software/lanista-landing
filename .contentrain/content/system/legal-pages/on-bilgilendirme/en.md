## 7. Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days from the day the contract is formed, without giving any reason and without paying any penalty.

**How do I give notice of withdrawal?** It is sufficient to send an email to info@lanista.com.tr or send written notice to the address above within the period. State your full name, the order date and the product name.

**Refund:** The full amount you paid is refunded to the card you paid with within 14 days at the latest after your notice of withdrawal reaches us. The time it takes for the money to reach your account depends on your bank.

## 8. Cases where the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised

Under Article 15/1 of the Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised in the following cases:

- **Subparagraph (ğ):** Services performed instantly in electronic form and intangible goods delivered instantly. For example, SMS credit loaded to your account.
- **Subparagraph (h):** Services whose performance begins with your approval before the withdrawal period ends.

On the confirmation page, by ticking a separate box, you approve the statement "I request that the service start immediately, before the withdrawal period ends; I accept that I will lose my right of withdrawal." If you do not give this approval, setup will not start.

Setup starts when our team opens your account or begins working with you, on site or remotely, for the setup. **Once setup has started, your right of withdrawal ends.** If setup has not started, you can withdraw within 14 days and get all your money back.

## 9. Merchant and tradesperson buyers

If you buy on behalf of your business for commercial or professional purposes, you are generally not considered a consumer within the meaning of 6502 sayılı Kanun (Consumer Protection Law No. 6502), and no statutory right of withdrawal arises. Lanista nevertheless applies the same refund procedure, in good faith, to withdrawal requests made before setup starts.
