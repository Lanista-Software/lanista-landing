This page explains how the software plans sold by LANİSTA YAZILIM SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Lanista") on lanista.com.tr are delivered, how an order can be cancelled and how money is refunded. Details are set out in the Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (Pre-Contractual Information Form) and the Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi (Distance Sales Contract).

## 1. What do we sell?

Lanista sells cloud-based business apps for small and local businesses. The product currently on sale is **Hesap Takip**.

**Nalbur Takip** and **Kasa Takip** are not on sale yet. No payment is taken for these products; only waiting list registration is available. People on the waiting list are notified when a product goes on sale.

Plans on sale (excluding VAT; 20% VAT is added):

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Plan | TRY 15,000 / year | Setup, customer list import, one year of use, maintenance, updates and support |
| Setup + 1 year of use | TRY 25,000 | Setup, customer list import, first year of use, maintenance, updates and support |
| Annual renewal | TRY 7,500 / year | After "Setup + 1 year", one more year of use, maintenance and support from the second year onwards |
| SMS pack | On request | Reminder SMS messages for overdue receivables |

## 2. How is delivery made?

Delivery is **entirely digital**. No physical product is shipped and no delivery fee is charged.

1. Once your payment is confirmed, an order confirmation is sent to you by email.
2. Our team calls you and schedules a day for setup.
3. Setup is carried out on site or remotely: your account is opened, your customer list is imported, the app is added to your devices and basic use is demonstrated.
4. When setup is complete, your account is activated. Your one-year term of use starts on this day.

**Delivery time:** Setup is completed within {{kurulum_suresi}} business days at the latest after your payment is confirmed.

If a delay is caused by you (such as missing information or a postponed appointment), the period is extended accordingly. If we cannot complete the setup within the period for a reason attributable to us, you can cancel the order and get back the full amount you paid.

## 3. Order cancellation and right of withdrawal

### If setup has not started

If you are a consumer, you can cancel your order without giving any reason **within 14 days** from the day the contract is formed (right of withdrawal). If setup has not yet started, the full amount you paid is refunded. No deductions are made.

### If setup has started

Article 15/1 of the Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği (Distance Contracts Regulation) removes the right of withdrawal in these two cases:

- **Subparagraph (h):** Services started with your explicit approval before the withdrawal period ends.
- **Subparagraph (ğ):** Services performed instantly in electronic form and intangible goods delivered instantly.

Before payment, on the confirmation page, you confirm with a separate box that you want the service to start immediately and that you will lose your right of withdrawal once setup has started. Setup does not start without this approval.

Setup starts when our team opens your account or begins working with you, on site or remotely, for the setup. From that moment, the right of withdrawal can no longer be exercised.

### Merchant and tradesperson buyers

If you bought on behalf of your business for commercial purposes, you are generally not considered a consumer and no statutory right of withdrawal arises. Even so, we apply the same refund procedure, in good faith, to cancellation requests received before setup starts.

## 4. How do I request a cancellation?

- Send an email to info@lanista.com.tr, or
- Send written notice to Bardakçı Mah. Teknokent Sk. No: 1 İç Kapı No: 9 Tuşba / Van.

It is sufficient to state your name or company name, the order date and the product name. We will confirm by email that your request has reached us. For questions, you can call {{telefon}}.

## 5. How and when is money refunded?

- Refunds are made **to the card you paid with**. No refunds are made in cash or to another account.
- The refund is initiated **within 14 days at the latest** from the day your cancellation notice reaches us.
- The time it takes for the money to appear on your card depends on your bank.
- For instalment payments, the refund may be reflected on your card in instalments, depending on the bank's practice.
- A return invoice is issued or the invoice is cancelled for the refunded amount.

## 6. SMS pack

- The SMS pack is not included in Hesap Takip plans and is sold separately.
- SMS credit is loaded to your account after purchase. Loaded SMS credit is a service delivered instantly and, under MSY (Distance Contracts Regulation) Art. 15/1-(ğ), is not covered by the right of withdrawal.
- If you cancel before the credit is loaded to your account, the amount you paid is refunded.
- Reminder SMS messages are only for payment reminders; no advertising is added to the messages.

## 7. Renewal

- **Plans do not renew automatically.** Your card is not stored and no automatic charges are made.
- We remind you before your term of use ends.
- If you want to continue, you pay the renewal fee again. Renewal is also made with a new order, using the same approval and payment steps.
- Renewal prices may be updated each year taking into account TÜFE/ÜFE (consumer and producer price index) rates. The current price is communicated to you before payment.
- If you do not renew, use of your account stops at the end of the term. You can request an export of your data before the term ends.

## 8. If there is a problem with the service

If you see an error in the app, contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email. We fix errors free of charge throughout the term of use. If you are unable to receive the service you paid for because of Lanista and we cannot resolve the problem within a reasonable time, you can contact us about a refund of the amount corresponding to the unused period.

## 9. Complaints and disputes

You can send your complaints to info@lanista.com.tr. If you are a consumer, you can apply to the Tüketici Hakem Heyeti (Consumer Arbitration Committee) within the monetary limits set each year by the Ticaret Bakanlığı (Ministry of Trade), and to the Tüketici Mahkemesi (Consumer Court) above these limits.
