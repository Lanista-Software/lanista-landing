This policy explains in plain language how LANİSTA YAZILIM SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Lanista", "we") protects your privacy on lanista.com.tr and in our apps. Legal details are in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice under the Personal Data Protection Law), and details about cookies are in the Cookie Policy.

## In short

- We collect only the data our business needs.
- We do not sell or rent your data.
- We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by iyzico.
- Analytics cookies do not run without your consent.
- The customer information you enter into the app is yours. We only store it on your behalf.
- To view, correct or delete your data, write to info@lanista.com.tr.

## 1. What information do we collect?

**Information you give us.** When you fill in the contact, demo or waiting list form, we receive your name, business name, phone number, email address and message. When you make a purchase, we collect your legal name, tax number or T.C. identity number (Turkish national ID number) and address for invoicing.

**Information created when you use the app.** Your account details, sign-in records and the sales, payment and customer records you enter into the app.

**Information created when you browse the site.** Only if you give consent, Google Analytics collects statistics such as which pages you visit and your device and browser details.

**Payment information.** You enter your card details on iyzico's payment page. This information never reaches us. We only see whether the payment succeeded and the amount.

## 2. What do we use this information for?

- To answer your questions and give demos,
- To take your order, carry out the setup and issue invoices,
- To run, support and improve the app,
- To remind you about renewal before your plan expires,
- To let you know when a product on the waiting list goes on sale,
- To meet our legal obligations.

We ask for your separate consent before sending you advertising and promotional messages. You can still buy our products without giving this consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

## 3. Your customers' information in the app

In products such as Hesap Takip, you enter your own customers' names, phone numbers, licence plates and debt details. This information is yours:

- You are the data controller for this data. As data processor, we only store and process it on your behalf.
- We do not use this information for our own purposes and do not share it with other businesses.
- In the shared parts catalogue, only part names and codes are shared. Your prices, customers and sales quantities are not shared.
- If you choose to send reminder SMS messages, they are sent only according to the rules you set, through our SMS provider İleti Merkezi.
- It is your responsibility to obtain your customers' phone numbers lawfully and to inform them.

## 4. Who do we share your information with?

We share it only with the following parties we work with to provide the service, and only as much as necessary:

- **iyzico:** payment processing,
- **Contentrain Studio:** storing submissions from the site's forms,
- **Google Firebase:** hosting app data (Europe region),
- **Google Analytics:** site statistics, only with your consent,
- **İleti Merkezi:** sending reminder SMS messages,
- **Our accountant:** accounting and tax matters,
- **Competent authorities:** where required by law.

Some service providers' servers may be located abroad. These transfers are carried out in accordance with KVKK Art. 9. Details are in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice).

## 5. How do we protect your information?

- The site and the app run only over HTTPS (encrypted connection).
- App data is kept on Google Firebase infrastructure, on servers in the Europe region. Because the data is stored in the cloud, it is not lost even if you lose your phone.
- Access to accounts is protected by user sign-in. Each business sees only its own data.
- Within our team, only people who need the data for their work can access it.
- We sign contracts that include confidentiality obligations with our service providers.

No system is 100% secure. If a security breach occurs, we will notify the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu (Personal Data Protection Board) and the affected people as soon as possible, as required by law.

Please do not share your password with anyone, and sign out of shared devices in your shop when you are done.

## 6. How long do we keep your information?

- **Form submissions:** Up to 2 years after our last contact.
- **Waiting list:** Until the product goes on sale or you leave the list.
- **Sales and invoice records:** For the period required by tax and commercial laws (up to 10 years).
- **App data:** For as long as the contract continues. When the contract ends, we hand your data over to you if you wish, and then delete it within a reasonable time.
- **Analytics data:** Up to 14 months.

## 7. Children's privacy

Our products are designed for businesses. The site and apps are not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we find out we have received such data, we delete it.

## 8. Your rights

You can find out whether your data is being processed, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. We answer your requests free of charge within 30 days at the latest. How to apply is explained in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice).

You can change your cookie preference at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of the site.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our services or the law change. The current version is always published on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top changes. If there is a significant change, we will also inform our customers by email.
