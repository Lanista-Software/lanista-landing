## 2. What data do we process, for what purpose and on what legal basis?

### 2.1. Contact and demo form

- **Data:** Full name, business name, phone number, email address, your message, the product you are interested in.
- **Purpose:** Answering your question, giving a demo, sending quotes and information.
- **Legal basis:** Processing is directly related to the formation of a contract (Art. 5/2-c) and our legitimate interest in communicating with you (Art. 5/2-f).

### 2.2. Waiting list

- **Data:** Full name, business name, phone number, email address, the product you are interested in.
- **Purpose:** Letting you know when products not yet on sale (Nalbur Takip, Kasa Takip) become available, and gauging demand.
- **Legal basis:** Processing is directly related to the formation of a contract (Art. 5/2-c) and our legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f). You can leave the waiting list at any time.

We obtain your separate, optional consent before sending commercial electronic messages (promotions, marketing). This consent is not a condition of purchase.

### 2.3. Sales, payment and invoicing

- **Data:** Full name or company name, T.C. identity number (Turkish national ID number) or tax number, tax office, address, phone number, email address, order details, payment result, approval records (date, time, document version), billing details.
- **Purpose:** Taking the order, forming and performing the contract, carrying out the setup, issuing invoices, sending renewal reminders, providing support, meeting legal retention obligations and protecting our rights in any dispute.
- **Legal basis:** Formation and performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c), compliance with our legal obligations (Art. 5/2-ç; tax and commercial legislation), and the establishment, exercise or protection of a right (Art. 5/2-e).

Your card details are not passed to Lanista. Payments are processed by iyzico, which processes card data under its own responsibility.

### 2.4. App account (the business that is our customer)

- **Data:** Full names of the account holder and users, phone number, email address, sign-in records, device and usage logs.
- **Purpose:** Opening the account, providing secure sign-in, delivering the service, fixing errors and providing support.
- **Legal basis:** Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c) and our legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 5/2-f). Obligations arising from 5651 sayılı Kanun (Law No. 5651 on internet publications) for access logs (Art. 5/2-ç).

### 2.5. Customer data within the app (our role as data processor)

Businesses enter their own customers' names, phone numbers, vehicle licence plates, and sales, debt and payment details into the app. **For this data, the business using the app is the data controller. Lanista is the data processor.** We process this data only on the business's instructions and to provide the service. We do not use this data for our own purposes and do not sell it.

If the business chooses, SMS reminders are sent to its customers about overdue debts. These SMS messages are sent through İleti Merkezi. Reminder SMS messages are informational and contain no advertising.

If you are a customer of one of these businesses and have a request about your data, you should first contact that business. We forward any requests we receive to the relevant business.

In the shared parts catalogue, only part names and codes are shared. Price, customer and sales information is not shared between businesses.

### 2.6. Cookies and site usage

- **Data:** Your cookie preference; if you consent, the pages you visit, device and browser information, approximate location and cookie ID.
- **Purpose:** Remembering your preference; if you consent, measuring and improving how the site is used.
- **Legal basis:** Our legitimate interest for storing your cookie preference (Art. 5/2-f). **Your explicit consent** for Google Analytics (Art. 5/1). Analytics cookies do not run without your consent. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

## 4. Transfers abroad

The servers or group companies of some service providers we use (such as Google Firebase, Google Analytics and the form infrastructure) may be located abroad. App data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the Europe region. Transfers made because of these services are carried out under KVKK Art. 9 by signing the standard contracts announced by the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu (Personal Data Protection Board) and notifying the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (Personal Data Protection Authority) of these contracts within the period set out in the legislation. Transfers relating to Google Analytics additionally require your cookie consent.

## 5. How do we collect data?

We collect your data through the following channels, by automated or partly automated means:

- The contact, demo and waiting list forms on the site,
- The order confirmation page and the iyzico payment page,
- Information you provide during setup,
- Use of the app,
- Phone, WhatsApp and email correspondence,
- Cookies (analytics cookies only with your consent).

## 6. How long do we keep it?

- Form submissions: Up to 2 years after our last contact. Deleted earlier if you request it.
- Waiting list: Until the product goes on sale or you leave the list.
- Sales, invoice and contract records: For the retention periods set out in tax and commercial legislation (up to 10 years).
- App data: For the term of the contract. When the contract ends, it is exported at the business's request and deleted within a reasonable time.
- Analytics data: Up to 14 months in Google Analytics.

When the period expires, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

## 7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to Lanista, you have the right to:

- Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
- Request information about it if it has been processed,
- Learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
- Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom your data has been transferred,
- Request correction if the data is incomplete or inaccurate,
- Request deletion or destruction of the data under the conditions in KVKK Art. 7,
- Request that the third parties to whom the data was transferred be notified of any correction or deletion,
- Object to an outcome against you resulting from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems,
- Claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

You can withdraw your cookie consent at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of the site.

In your application, clearly state your full name, T.C. identity number (or, if you are a foreign national, your nationality and passport number), your address for notifications, your email address and phone number if any, and your request.

We will conclude your application free of charge within **30 days** at the latest. If the process involves an additional cost, the fee schedule set by the Board may apply. If your application is rejected or you find our response insufficient, you can file a complaint with the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu (Personal Data Protection Board).
