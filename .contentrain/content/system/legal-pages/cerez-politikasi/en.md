This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies LANİSTA YAZILIM SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Lanista") uses on lanista.com.tr, why we use them and how you can change your preferences. General information about your personal data is set out in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice under the Personal Data Protection Law).

## 1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file saved in your browser when you visit a website. The site can read this file on your next visit. The browser's "local storage" (localStorage) can also be used for similar purposes. In this policy we refer to both as "cookies".

Cookies fall into two groups:

- **First-party cookies:** Placed directly by lanista.com.tr.
- **Third-party cookies:** Placed by another service we use on the site (for example, Google Analytics).

## 2. Which cookies do we use?

Our site uses two types of cookies.

### 2.1. Strictly necessary cookies

These are needed for the site to work and to remember your choice. No consent is requested for them, but if you block them you will be asked for your cookie preference on every visit.

By default, that is, before you make a choice, our site **places no cookies at all.** When you make a choice, only that choice is saved in your browser's local storage.

### 2.2. Analytics cookies

These help us understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, where visitors come from and how long they stay on a page. We use this information as aggregate statistics to improve the site.

We use Google Analytics 4 for this purpose. **Analytics cookies are loaded only when you click "Accept".** If you click "Reject" or make no choice, Google Analytics does not run.

We do not use advertising, retargeting or social media tracking cookies.

## 3. Cookie table

| Name | Category | Provider | Purpose | Duration |

The analytics cookies in the table are placed only with your consent. The name of the `_ga_*` cookie contains our site's measurement ID.

## 4. Payment page

When you click "Proceed to payment", you are redirected to iyzico's payment page. This page belongs to iyzico. iyzico may use its own cookies to keep the payment secure. These cookies are subject to iyzico's own policies and are not placed by lanista.com.tr.

## 5. How to manage your preferences

**Cookie banner.** On your first visit, a cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen. The "Accept" and "Reject" options are equally easy to choose. No analytics cookies are loaded until you make your choice.

**Changing your preference.** You can change your decision at any time by clicking the **"Cookie preferences"** link at the very bottom of the site. When you withdraw your consent, Google Analytics stops running from the next page onwards. You can delete any analytics cookies already placed from your browser.

**Browser settings.** You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings:

- Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- Safari: Settings > Safari > Advanced > Website Data (iPhone); Safari > Settings > Privacy (Mac)
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

If you clear your browser data, your cookie preference is also deleted and the banner appears again on your next visit.

**Opting out of Google Analytics.** You can turn off Google Analytics measurement on all websites with Google's "Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on".

## 6. Legal basis

- Storing your cookie preference is necessary for the site to function and to respect your choice. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
- The legal basis for analytics cookies is **your explicit consent** (KVKK Art. 5/1). You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Data collected through Google Analytics may be transferred to Google's servers abroad. This transfer is carried out in accordance with KVKK Art. 9. Details are in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice).

## 7. Our apps

Our apps, such as Lanista Auto Parts, store strictly necessary technical data on your device (session information, offline entry queue) to keep you signed in and to work offline. This data is required for the app to function. No advertising or tracking cookies are used in our apps.

## 8. Changes

If we add a new cookie or the purpose of an existing cookie changes, we will update this policy and the table. If a change requires new consent, the cookie banner will be shown to you again.
