## Article 2 – Subject of the contract

The subject of this contract is the sale, setup and activation of the software service purchased by the Buyer. The rights and obligations of the parties are set out in this contract.

Purchased in this order:

- Product: {{urun}}

## Article 3 – Price

3.1. Prices are shown in Turkish lira, excluding VAT, and are marked "+ VAT". The confirmation page shows the price excluding VAT, the 20% VAT amount and the total including VAT separately.

3.2. The total price of the plan that is the subject of this contract is {{tutar}}, taxes included. Since delivery is digital, no separate delivery fee is charged.

3.3. The price valid at the time of the order does not change during the paid term of the plan.

3.4. Renewal prices may be updated each year taking into account TÜFE/ÜFE (consumer and producer price index) rates. The current renewal price is communicated to the Buyer before the renewal payment. If the Buyer does not accept the new price, the Buyer is under no obligation to renew.

3.5. The SMS pack is not included in the plan and is sold separately. The price of the SMS pack is provided on request, and it is purchased with a separate order.

## Article 4 – Payment

4.1. Payment is made by debit or credit card through the iyzico payment infrastructure. Card details are not passed to Lanista and are not stored by Lanista.

4.2. Instalment options are shown on the iyzico payment page according to the Buyer's card. For instalment payments, the bank's terms apply.

4.3. Once payment is complete, an order confirmation is sent to the Buyer by email. This contract and the Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (Pre-Contractual Information Form) are attached to the email as PDFs.

4.4. After payment, Lanista issues an e-Arşiv invoice (electronic archive invoice) in the Buyer's name and sends it to the Buyer's email address.

## Article 5 – Delivery and account activation

5.1. Delivery is digital. No physical product is shipped.

5.2. Lanista completes the setup and activates the Buyer's account within {{kurulum_suresi}} business days at the latest after the payment is confirmed. Setup is carried out on site or remotely at a time agreed with the Buyer.

5.3. Setup includes opening the account, importing the customer list provided by the Buyer, adding the app to the Buyer's devices and demonstrating basic use.

5.4. If the setup is delayed for reasons attributable to the Buyer (such as not providing information or missing an appointment), the period is extended accordingly. If the setup cannot be completed within the period for a reason attributable to Lanista, the Buyer may terminate the contract. In that case, the full amount paid is refunded in the manner set out in Article 6.5.

5.5. The term of use starts on the day the account is activated and ends at the end of the plan term (one year).

## Article 6 – Right of withdrawal

6.1. If the Buyer is a consumer, the Buyer may withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving any reason and without paying any penalty. For service contracts, this period starts on the day the contract is formed.

6.2. Notice of withdrawal is given within the period by email to info@lanista.com.tr or in writing to the address in Article 1. It is sufficient for the notice to state the full name, order date and product name.

6.3. **Cases where the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised.** Under Article 15/1 of the Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for:

- Subparagraph (ğ): Services performed instantly in electronic form and intangible goods delivered instantly to the consumer (such as activating the software or loading SMS credit to the account),
- Subparagraph (h): Services whose performance begins with the consumer's approval before the withdrawal period ends.

6.4. On the confirmation page, by ticking a separate checkbox, the Buyer requests that the service start immediately before the withdrawal period ends and accepts that the right of withdrawal will be lost once setup has started. Setup does not start without this approval. Setup is deemed to have started when the Lanista team opens the Buyer's account or begins working with the Buyer, on site or remotely, for the setup.

6.5. **If setup has not started,** the Buyer may withdraw within 14 days. In that case, the full amount paid is refunded to the card used for payment within 14 days at the latest from the day the notice of withdrawal reaches Lanista. For instalment payments, the refund may be reflected on the card in instalments, depending on the bank's practice. The time it takes for the money to reach the account depends on the bank.

6.6. If the Buyer purchases as a merchant or tradesperson for commercial or professional purposes, the Buyer is generally not considered a consumer within the meaning of Law No. 6502, and no statutory right of withdrawal arises. Lanista nevertheless applies the refund in Article 6.5, in good faith, to withdrawal requests from such Buyers made before setup starts.

## Article 7 – Term and renewal

7.1. Plans are fixed-term contracts of one year.

7.2. **Plans do not renew automatically.** Lanista does not store the Buyer's card and makes no automatic charges.

7.3. Before the term of use ends, Lanista sends the Buyer a reminder by email, SMS or phone. A Buyer who wishes to continue places a new order by paying the renewal fee again.

7.4. A Buyer who purchased the "Setup + 1 year" plan may continue from the second year at the "Annual renewal" price. A Buyer who purchased the "Annual Plan" continues at the current price of the same plan.

7.5. If the term ends and no renewal is made, the account may be made read-only or suspended. The Buyer may request an export of their data before the term ends.

## Article 8 – Lanista's obligations

- To activate the account within the period in Article 5,
- To provide the product with the features presented as part of the plan,
- To provide maintenance, security updates and support throughout the term of use,
- To keep the Buyer's data confidential and use it only to provide the service,
- To announce planned maintenance in advance wherever possible.

Lanista is not liable for circumstances beyond its control, such as internet connectivity, device failures and outages in third-party infrastructure. The app continues to record entries when there is no connection and sends the data once the connection returns.

## Article 9 – The Buyer's obligations

- To provide accurate information for the order and setup,
- To protect the account password and sign-in details, and to notify Lanista immediately of any unauthorised use,
- To be responsible for the accuracy of the information entered into the app (such as customers, debts and phone numbers),
- If sending SMS reminders to customers, to have obtained those customers' phone numbers lawfully,
- Not to use the product for unlawful purposes, and not to copy, reverse engineer or rent the software to others.

## Article 10 – Personal data

10.1. The Buyer's order, billing and contact data is processed as described in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice under the Personal Data Protection Law).

10.2. For the data of the Buyer's own customers entered into the app, the Buyer is the data controller and Lanista is the data processor. Lanista processes this data only on the Buyer's behalf and to provide the service.

## Article 11 – Dispute resolution

11.1. The Buyer may first send complaints to info@lanista.com.tr. Lanista responds to complaints as soon as possible.

11.2. Consumer Buyers may apply to the Tüketici Hakem Heyeti (Consumer Arbitration Committee) where they live or where the transaction took place, within the monetary limits set each year by the Ticaret Bakanlığı (Ministry of Trade). Applications can also be made via e-Devlet (Turkey's e-Government portal). For disputes above these limits, the Tüketici Mahkemeleri (Consumer Courts) have jurisdiction. Provisions on mediation as a precondition for filing a lawsuit are reserved.

11.3. In disputes where the Buyer is not a consumer, the courts and enforcement offices of Van have jurisdiction.

## Article 12 – Evidence and notices

In correspondence between the parties, email and Lanista's system records (approval time, contract version, payment record) may be used as evidence. Notices are sent to the addresses in Article 1.

## Article 13 – Entry into force

On the confirmation page, the Buyer confirms with separate checkboxes that they have read the Pre-Contractual Information Form and this contract, and clicks "Confirm and proceed to payment". The contract is formed and enters into force upon completion of payment. A copy of the contract is sent to the Buyer's email address. This contract consists of 13 articles.
