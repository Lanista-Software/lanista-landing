In accordance with Article 7 of the Elektronik Ticarette Hizmet Sağlayıcı ve Aracı Hizmet Sağlayıcılar Hakkında Yönetmelik (Regulation on Service Providers and Intermediary Service Providers in Electronic Commerce), this guide explains step by step how to place an order on lanista.com.tr. Seller: LANİSTA YAZILIM SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Lanista").

## 1. Ordering steps

### Step 1 – Choose the product and plan

On the product page you can see the plans, what they include and their prices. Prices are shown excluding VAT and marked "+ VAT". Click the "Buy" button under the plan you want to purchase.

Only products that are on sale can be purchased. No payment is taken for products shown as "Coming soon" or "Pilot"; you can only join the waiting list.

If you would like to see the product before buying, fill in the "Request a demo" form. Our team will show you the product.

### Step 2 – Enter your details and accept the documents

The "Buy" button takes you to Lanista's order confirmation page. On this page:

1. You see the **order summary**: product, plan, term, price excluding VAT, VAT amount and total including VAT.
2. You enter your full name or company name, phone number, email address and billing details.
3. You read the **Ön Bilgilendirme Formu (Pre-Contractual Information Form)** and the **Mesafeli Satış Sözleşmesi (Distance Sales Contract)**. These documents show the product, plan and amount you selected.
4. You tick three separate checkboxes. The boxes are unticked by default:
   - I have read and approve the Pre-Contractual Information Form.
   - I have read and accept the Distance Sales Contract.
   - I request that the service start immediately, before the withdrawal period ends; I accept that I will lose my right of withdrawal.
5. If you wish, you can give separate consent to receive promotional and announcement messages. This consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.
6. You click **"Confirm and proceed to payment"**. Clicking this button means you are entering into an obligation to pay.

Your approval is recorded together with the date, time and document version.

### Step 3 – Pay with iyzico

You are redirected to iyzico's secure payment page, where you enter your card details. Instalment options are shown on this page according to your card. Your card details never reach Lanista and are not stored.

### Step 4 – Receive the confirmation email

Once payment is complete, an order confirmation is sent to you by email. The email includes:

- The order summary and the amount paid,
- The Pre-Contractual Information Form and the Distance Sales Contract (PDF attachments),
- Next steps for setup.

After payment, an e-Arşiv invoice (electronic archive invoice) is issued in your name and sent to your email address.

### Step 5 – Setup and getting started

Our team calls you and schedules a day for setup. Setup is completed on site or remotely within {{kurulum_suresi}} business days at the latest after your payment is confirmed. Your account is opened, your customer list is imported and the app is added to your devices. Your term of use starts on the day your account is opened.

## 2. How to correct input errors

- You can review and change all the information on the confirmation page before clicking "Confirm and proceed to payment".
- If you selected the wrong plan, you can go back to the product page with your browser's "back" button or the link on the page and choose another plan.
- If a required field is empty or invalid (for example, an invalid email address), the page warns you and does not let you continue.
- On the iyzico payment page, you can correct your card details or cancel the payment before completing it. If you close the page without completing payment, no order is created.
- If you notice an error in your billing or contact details after payment, write to info@lanista.com.tr or call {{telefon}}. We will correct the information and reissue the invoice if necessary.

## 3. How the contract is stored and how you can access it

- The Pre-Contractual Information Form and the Distance Sales Contract are sent to your email address **as PDFs** after payment. We recommend that you keep this email.
- The current versions of these documents are always published on our website, in the legal links at the bottom of the page.
- Lanista keeps the version of the documents you approved and your approval record for the legally required retention periods.
- If you need another copy of the contract later, simply write to info@lanista.com.tr. It will be sent to your registered email address.

The contract language is Turkish.

## 4. How renewal works

Plans do not renew automatically. Your card is not stored and no automatic charges are made. We remind you before your term ends. If you want to continue, you buy the renewal plan by following the steps above.

## 5. Cancellation and refunds

If setup has not started, you can cancel your order within 14 days and receive a full refund to your card. Once setup has started with your approval, the right of withdrawal can no longer be exercised. Details are on the "Delivery, Cancellation and Refund Terms" page.

## 6. Privacy rules

The information you provide when ordering is processed in accordance with the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Privacy Notice under the Personal Data Protection Law) and the Privacy Policy. Analytics cookies on our site run only with your consent; details are in the Cookie Policy.

## 7. Complaints and alternative dispute resolution

1. **Write to us:** Send your complaint to info@lanista.com.tr or call {{telefon}}. We will first try to resolve the issue together with you.
2. **Tüketici Hakem Heyeti (Consumer Arbitration Committee):** If you are a consumer, you can apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee where you live or where the transaction took place, within the monetary limits set each year by the Ticaret Bakanlığı (Ministry of Trade). Applications can also be made via e-Devlet (Turkey's e-Government portal).
3. **Mediation and the Tüketici Mahkemesi (Consumer Court):** For disputes above the monetary limit, you can apply first to a mediator, where required by law, and then to the Consumer Court.
