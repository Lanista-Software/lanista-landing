Lanista is a software company based in Van Teknokent (Van Technopark). We have been building software since 2019. Today, the core of our business is ready-made business apps for small and local businesses. We sell them at clear, fixed prices, and we handle the setup for you.

Our registered legal name is LANİSTA YAZILIM SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ.

## What we do

### 1. Ready-made apps for local businesses

Many small businesses still run on a paper ledger or an Excel sheet. It gets hard to keep track of who owes what, overdue payments are forgotten, and calling customers to remind them is awkward. For these businesses we build simple, sector-specific apps that run on a phone.

**Hesap Takip** (on sale) — A credit sales and customer account app for auto parts shops and repair-district businesses. A sale is entered in 20–30 seconds. Payments, returns and due dates are tracked. Overdue receivables show up on your phone every morning. If you wish, the system sends reminder SMS messages to your customers. Statements are sent over WhatsApp. A vehicle's history opens with one tap by licence plate.

- Annual Plan: TRY 15,000 + VAT / year
- Setup + 1 year of use: TRY 25,000 + VAT; from the second year, TRY 7,500 + VAT / year
- SMS packs are sold separately.

Plans do not renew automatically. We remind you before your term ends; if you want to continue, you simply pay again.

**Nalbur Takip** (coming soon) — For hardware stores, tool shops and building material suppliers. Sales and statements per job site, recipient signatures on delivery, and cheque and promissory note tracking.

**Kasa Takip** (in pilot) — A daily cash book for kiosks, cafés and small restaurants. Revenue by sales channel, platform commissions, expenses and real profit.

We do not take payment for products that are coming soon or in pilot. You can join the waiting list for these products and be the first to hear when they go on sale.

All our apps are built on the same foundation:

- **They run on your phone.** There is no software to install. The app is added to your phone's home screen. You can also sign in with the same account from the computer or tablet in your shop.
- **Your data lives in the cloud.** Records are stored on Google Firebase servers in Europe. If you lose your phone, you do not lose your data.
- **They keep working offline.** Entries are saved even when the connection drops and are sent once it comes back.
- **We do the setup.** We open your account, import your customer list and set up the app on site or remotely.

### 2. Custom software development

The team behind our ready-made products has spent years building web and mobile software for companies and startups. When a ready-made product is not enough, we design and develop software end to end. This experience also helps us make our own products more robust.

## How we work

**Transparent pricing.** Our prices are listed on the website. There are no surprise fees. If something is sold separately, such as SMS, we say so clearly.

**No automatic renewal.** We do not store your card and never charge you without notice. We remind you before your term ends; whether to continue is your decision.

**Simple products.** We build screens simple enough for a shopkeeper to use every day. We do not add features nobody needs.

**Tested in the field.** We trial every new product with real businesses first. We do not put a product on sale without enough demand, and we never take payment for a product that does not exist yet.

**Respect for your data.** The information you enter into the app is yours. We never share your prices, customers or sales with anyone.

**Support close at hand.** You can reach us by phone and WhatsApp. If you are in Van, we do the setup in your shop.
